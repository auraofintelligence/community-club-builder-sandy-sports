AI drafts. Humans decide.

An AI-first club is not a machine-run club. It is a club where the boring admin has a system, people keep agency, and sensitive decisions stay human.

Four layers Privacy posture Weekly loop

The operating system is ordinary on purpose.

Shared drive, shared email, forms, registers, calendars and templates are enough to start. AI sits on top as a drafting and sorting layer, not as an authority.

Public

Website, event calendar, waitlist, public updates and evidence notes.

Operations

Members, volunteers, rosters, finance, safety, assets and reminders.

Knowledge

Rules, policies, minutes, agreements, grant notes and handover records.

AI

Agenda drafts, minutes summaries, grant first drafts, social options and missing-field checks.

Review

Human gates before money, safety, complaints, cultural claims or public commitments.

Consent

Less personal data, clearer permissions and no sensitive uploads into casual tools.

Privacy is part of the architecture.

OAIC guidance says privacy obligations still apply when AI products handle personal information. For this club, the practical move is to keep sensitive data out of casual AI prompts, remove names where possible and review all AI output before it becomes a decision.

Forms collect the facts

Join, volunteer, incident, media consent, asset and grant evidence forms feed clean records.

AI drafts the summary

The digital admin lead receives a plain weekly summary of open actions, missing fields, due dates and decisions needed.

Committee chooses

People decide the trade-offs. The system remembers the decision and reminds the right owner.
