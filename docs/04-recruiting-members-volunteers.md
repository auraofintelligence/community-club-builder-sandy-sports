# Recruiting Members And Volunteers

Recruiting is not just "get numbers". It is finding the right people for the right level of responsibility.

## Member Types

Start simple:

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary member | Can vote, attend, play and help |
| Junior member | Can participate with guardian consent |
| Family member | Makes it easier for households to join |
| Volunteer member | Helps at events without needing to play |
| Supporter member | Local business, elder, alumni or friend |

Keep the first version easy. A confusing membership model creates admin mud.

## Volunteer Ladder

Give people small doors before big jobs:

1. Turn up
2. Help pack down
3. Join the volunteer list
4. Take one event shift
5. Own one recurring checklist
6. Join a subcommittee
7. Nominate for committee

This protects the club from giving keys to people before trust is real.

## First Recruiting Message

Short version:

> We are forming a community sand sports club for Straddie / Minjerribah: mixed ages, mixed genders, family-friendly, low-pressure and built for wellbeing. The first job is not big promises. It is forming a trustworthy founding group that can share admin, test small events and do things properly.

## Who To Invite First

Aim for a balanced founding circle:

- women and girls with sport, family, school, health or community insight
- existing local club people who understand volunteer reality
- young adults who can help with digital and social content
- parents and carers
- safety-minded people
- quiet organisers
- local business supporters
- people with grant, finance, legal, teaching, coaching, media or event experience

Do not stack the room with one friendship group.

## Onboarding Questions

Ask every serious volunteer:

- Which role sounds fun?
- Which role sounds boring but possible?
- Do you prefer people, paperwork, gear, safety, media or money?
- Can you do a tiny recurring task monthly?
- Do you consent to being in the shared volunteer register?
- Do you need accessibility, timing or family support to participate?

## Recognition

Do not pay people with vague praise.

Use visible recognition:

- volunteer of the month
- public thank-you with consent
- skill badges
- free event pass
- coffee voucher
- shirt after set number of shifts
- reference letter for young volunteers
- leadership pathway for women and girls

The trick is to make contribution feel seen without turning the club into a status game.
