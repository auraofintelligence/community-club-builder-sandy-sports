# AI-first Admin System

AI-first does not mean AI is in charge. It means the club designs admin so AI can reduce boring load from day one.

Human decides. AI drafts.

## The One Source Of Truth

Pick one shared drive and use it properly.

Suggested folders:

```text
00 Read Me First
01 Governance
02 Meetings
03 Members
04 Volunteers
05 Finance
06 Grants
07 Events
08 Safety
09 Marketing
10 Assets
11 AI Drafts
```

No private laptop should be the only place where club knowledge lives.

## The Basic Automation Stack

Start with common tools before fancy tools:

- shared email inbox
- online membership form
- volunteer form
- spreadsheet member register
- shared calendar
- meeting agenda template
- minutes template
- task board
- asset register
- incident form
- grant evidence log
- media consent form
- AI prompt library

## AI Jobs

Good AI jobs:

- turn rough notes into meeting minutes
- draft agendas from open tasks
- summarise feedback forms
- draft grant answers from approved evidence
- create social post options from event facts
- produce role checklists
- create reminder emails
- find missing fields in a spreadsheet
- write plain-English versions of policies

Bad AI jobs:

- deciding child safety matters
- inventing approvals
- handling private member data without consent
- sending messages without human review
- making cultural claims
- making legal or financial decisions
- pretending draft facts are verified

## Privacy Rule

Do not upload sensitive personal information to an AI tool unless the club has permission, a clear privacy basis, and understands where that data goes.

Safer pattern:

1. remove names, phone numbers, addresses and medical details
2. ask AI for a structure or summary
3. human checks it
4. store the final version in the club folder

## The Admin Weekly Loop

Every week:

1. Form responses land in the spreadsheet.
2. AI drafts a short admin summary.
3. Digital admin lead checks it.
4. Secretary adds decisions needed to the agenda.
5. Treasurer checks money items.
6. Volunteer lead checks roster gaps.
7. Committee sees one simple dashboard.

## Simple Dashboard

Track:

- active members
- junior members
- volunteers this month
- open tasks
- upcoming events
- safety items
- money in and out
- grants due
- thank-yous owed

If the dashboard takes more than 10 minutes to update, simplify it.
