# Milestones And Evidence Loops

A fast club does not run on fake certainty. It runs on clean loops that can move whenever people, care, proof, permission and support line up.

## The Loop

1. Observe what is actual.
2. Name the hypothesis.
3. Run the smallest honest test.
4. Record the evidence.
5. Decide what becomes a commitment.
6. Stop, repeat or apply for support.

## Actuals To Track

- incorporated status or pre-incorporation status
- committee decisions
- member and volunteer interest
- site permission status
- insurance status
- safety and child-safety requirements
- grant or tender rules
- partner conversations
- money committed and money only imagined

## Grant Evidence

- attendance
- return visits
- volunteer hours
- community benefit
- inclusion result
- safety result
- wellbeing signal
- photos or media with consent
- feedback and objections
- partner support
- budget and quotes
- next responsible step

## Tender Readiness

- who can legally contract
- who can deliver
- what insurance applies
- what capability is proven
- what risks belong to the club
- what risks belong to a partner
- what sits outside tender scope for now

## Responsible Speed

Rules, guidelines and deadlines matter. They are the published starting map.

Some programs also have discretion, staged pathways, exemptions or ways to ask a better question. Do not assume flexibility. Ask clearly, record the answer and get any permission in writing.

## Do Not Scale If

- one person is still carrying the system
- the committee cannot tell actuals from simulations
- money promises are ahead of approvals
- children or vulnerable people would be left inside an improvised system
- community support is being inferred rather than shown
- public language is claiming more than the club can prove
