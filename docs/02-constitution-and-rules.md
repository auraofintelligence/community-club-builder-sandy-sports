# Constitution And Rules Starter

Start from the Queensland Office of Fair Trading model rules unless the founding group has a clear reason and proper advice to customise.

Model rules guidance: <https://www.qld.gov.au/community/fair-trading/associations-charities-and-non-for-profits/incorporated-associations/running-an-incorporated-association/rules-for-associations>

## Objects

Draft objects for a Sandy Sports style club:

1. To provide inclusive, safe and affordable sand sport, recreation and wellbeing activities.
2. To create a welcoming meeting place for people of different ages, genders, backgrounds and abilities.
3. To support women, girls and gender-balanced leadership in community sport.
4. To support youth participation, family-friendly events and volunteer development.
5. To collaborate respectfully with Traditional Owner bodies, local clubs, businesses, schools, Council and sporting organisations.
6. To use digital tools and AI-assisted administration to reduce volunteer workload while protecting privacy and human decision-making.
7. To operate as a not-for-profit association for community benefit.

## Rules To Think About Carefully

Do not hide important culture settings in a side policy nobody reads. Decide them early.

### Leadership Balance

Suggested policy language:

> The association will actively promote women and girls into leadership and will aim for gender-balanced committee and subcommittee membership.

Suggested operating rule:

> The chair and deputy chair roles should not be held by two people with the same gender identity where a suitable alternative nomination is available.

Get proper advice before turning hard gender targets into binding constitutional clauses.

### Founder Limits

The founder can help start the club. The founder should not own the club.

Suggested operating rule:

> No single person may control the association's bank access, member list, website, social accounts, grant records, venue relationships or equipment register without committee visibility and at least one backup admin.

### Conflicts Of Interest

Suggested operating rule:

> Committee members must declare any personal, family, business, political, sponsor or property interest that could influence a decision. The declaration is recorded in the minutes before the decision is made.

### AI Use

Suggested operating rule:

> AI tools may assist with drafts, summaries, reminders, reporting and planning. AI output must be reviewed by a responsible human before publication, lodgement, payment, member action or safety decision.

### Child Safety

Suggested operating rule:

> The association will maintain child-safe procedures, blue card checks where required, incident reporting, consent records and supervision practices for junior activities.

### Cultural Respect

Suggested operating rule:

> The association will not use cultural names, stories, imagery, ceremony, Country-based claims or Traditional Owner endorsement without appropriate permission and guidance.

## Documents To Keep Beside The Constitution

The constitution is not enough. Keep these living documents:

- code of conduct
- child safety policy
- member protection policy
- conflict of interest register
- risk register
- volunteer role cards
- privacy and media consent process
- AI use policy
- finance and payment policy
- complaints process
- social media policy
