The club stays portable.

The Sandy Sports idea can reference Amity, Ballow Road, Point Lookout, grants, tenders and film pathways without trapping them all inside one fragile legal body.

The club is the social, sporting and volunteer vehicle. Site projects and capital works stay as relationships, charters or future agreements.

club first site later trust before scale

AI assists the system. It does not become the system.

Self-sovereign does not mean doing admin alone. The stronger pattern is consent-aware records, shared access, human decision gates and AI doing the first boring draft.

Public layer

Website, join form, event calendar, waitlist, updates and public evidence.

Operations layer

Members, volunteers, money, assets, safety, rosters and recurring reminders.

Knowledge layer

Rules, policies, minutes, agreements, grant notes and handover packs.

Leadership should feel inhabitable.

Mixed-gender and female-led leadership is not a badge stuck on top. It changes meeting times, role design, tone, safety culture and who gets to hold real authority.

The aim is not to recruit a new martyr. It is to build a container where capable people can step in, step back and still leave the club healthier.
