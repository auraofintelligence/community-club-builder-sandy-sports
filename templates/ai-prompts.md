# AI Prompt Library

Use these with approved notes and non-sensitive data.

## Meeting Agenda

```text
You are helping a small Queensland community sport club prepare a committee agenda.
Use Australian English.
Turn these notes into a short agenda with decisions needed, discussion items, action items and owners.
Do not invent facts or approvals.

Notes:
[paste notes]
```

## Minutes From Notes

```text
Turn these rough notes into plain minutes for a community club committee.
Separate decisions, action items, owners and due dates.
Flag anything that sounds like a legal, finance, child safety, insurance or site permission issue needing human review.

Notes:
[paste notes]
```

## Grant Evidence Summary

```text
Summarise this event evidence for a community grant report.
Use only the facts provided.
Do not exaggerate outcomes.
Include attendance, volunteer hours, community benefit, safety result, inclusion result and next step.

Evidence:
[paste evidence]
```

## Social Post Draft

```text
Draft three warm, local, non-salesy social posts for a community sand sports club.
Use only these facts.
Do not imply approvals, funding, partnerships or cultural endorsement unless stated.
Include a simple invitation and a thank-you.

Facts:
[paste facts]
```

## Policy Plain-English Rewrite

```text
Rewrite this club policy into plain Australian English for volunteers.
Keep the meaning.

Policy:
[paste policy]
```
