# Committee Role Cards

Use these as plain-English role cards. The goal is to stop mystery jobs.

## Chair Or Co-chair

Guards purpose and fair process.

Does:

- runs meetings
- keeps discussion on track
- makes sure quiet voices are invited in
- checks decisions match the club purpose
- helps resolve tension early

Does not:

- control everything
- make private deals
- own the club

## Secretary

Guards records.

Does:

- meeting notices
- agendas
- minutes
- member register
- official correspondence
- lodgement reminders

Needs a backup because records are the club memory.

## Treasurer

Guards money.

Does:

- budget
- payments
- receipts
- finance report
- bank reconciliation
- asset register
- annual finance records

Must not be the only person with bank visibility.

## Safety And Wellbeing Lead

Guards people.

Does:

- risk register
- incident process
- child safety process
- blue card tracking where required
- accessibility checks
- wellbeing culture

This role needs authority to slow an event down.

## Volunteer And Membership Lead

Guards belonging.

Does:

- onboarding
- rosters
- volunteer thank-yous
- member follow-up
- role matching
- women and girls participation check-ins

## Digital Admin Lead

Guards the system.

Does:

- shared folders
- forms
- spreadsheets
- calendars
- task board
- AI prompt library
- password handover process

Does not make governance decisions alone.

## Events And Sport Lead

Guards delivery.

Does:

- activity calendar
- gear needs
- coaching roster
- venue booking checklist
- event run sheet
- post-event debrief
