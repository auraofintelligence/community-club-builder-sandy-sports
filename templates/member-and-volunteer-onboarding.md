# Member And Volunteer Onboarding

Use this as a form brief.

## Member Fields

- Full name
- Preferred name
- Email
- Phone
- Suburb / township
- Membership type
- Emergency contact
- Age bracket
- Guardian details for junior members
- Consent to club communications
- Media consent
- Accessibility or support needs
- Interests
- Agreement to code of conduct

## Volunteer Fields

- Name
- Contact details
- Blue card status if relevant
- Areas of interest: people, sport, gear, food, admin, media, safety, grant evidence
- Available times
- Physical limits or support needs
- Emergency contact
- Consent to be added to volunteer register
- Any conflicts of interest to declare
