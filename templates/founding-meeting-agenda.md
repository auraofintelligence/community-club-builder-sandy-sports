# Founding Meeting Agenda

Purpose: hold a clean meeting so the group can decide whether to form and incorporate the club.

## Before The Meeting

- Confirm at least 7 people are willing to be founding members.
- Send the draft objects.
- Send the model rules link.
- Prepare nomination forms.
- Prepare conflict of interest declarations.
- Prepare this agenda.

## Agenda

1. Welcome and acknowledgement.
2. Why this club is being proposed.
3. Confirm attendance and founding member list.
4. Confirm the club is intended to be not-for-profit.
5. Discuss and approve the proposed name.
6. Discuss and approve the proposed objects.
7. Resolve to incorporate as a Queensland incorporated association.
8. Resolve to adopt the rules.
9. Elect management committee.
10. Appoint the person who will lodge the incorporation application.
11. Confirm first admin setup tasks.
12. Confirm next meeting date.

## Draft Resolution Wording

> That the members present resolve to form a not-for-profit association and apply for incorporation under Queensland law.

> That the association adopt the Office of Fair Trading model rules, with the association objects recorded in the application.

> That [name] is appointed to complete and lodge the incorporation application on behalf of the association.

## After The Meeting

- Save minutes in the shared folder.
- Save attendance.
- Save declared conflicts.
- Save committee role list.
- Lodge the application only after the committee checks the pack.
